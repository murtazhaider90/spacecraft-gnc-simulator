# Thermal & Airflow Engineering — Sanitized Case Study

## Context

Engineering work on high-power AI compute infrastructure required balancing thermal performance, pressure loss, recirculation risk, manufacturability, reliability and supplier constraints.

## Engineering approach

- Used CFD and CAD to compare airflow concepts and identify likely recirculation, bypass and hotspot risks.
- Built repeatable physical test setups with pressure, temperature and airflow sensing.
- Automated data capture using ESP32-based instrumentation and Python analysis.
- Compared simulation and rig measurements, then investigated discrepancies before changing designs or modelling assumptions.
- Used rapid prototyping to iterate ducting/sealing concepts while considering lead time, cost and manufacturability.

## What is intentionally excluded

No employer CAD, CFD files, sensor datasets, supplier information, dimensions, proprietary geometry or unpublished IP are included here. The companion `projects/airflow-test-instrumentation-rig` directory is an independent portfolio implementation of the general measurement/analysis workflow.

## Transferable engineering themes

Requirements decomposition, test planning, instrumentation, uncertainty awareness, model-to-test correlation, root-cause investigation, design iteration and cross-functional decision making.
