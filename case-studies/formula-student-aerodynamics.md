# Formula Student Aerodynamics — Sanitized Case Study

## Scope

Aerodynamic concept development within a multidisciplinary Formula Student team using CATIA and ANSYS Discovery CFD.

## Workflow

- Developed and modified aerodynamic component geometry in CAD.
- Compared CFD outputs across design iterations instead of judging concepts visually.
- Considered aerodynamic performance alongside packaging, manufacturability and vehicle integration.
- Communicated simulation-driven recommendations within a wider vehicle-development context.

No team-owned CAD, meshes, setup files, performance numbers or unreleased vehicle geometry are published here. The independent `projects/parametric-formula-wing` directory demonstrates the general parameter-sweep and result-analysis workflow using synthetic coefficients.
